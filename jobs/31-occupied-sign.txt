# A flip-flop: two transistors, each one's collector feeding the other's base through 10k, so
# whichever is on holds the other off, and stays so until a button grounds a base. PB2 grounds
# Q2's base: Q2 off, its collector high, Q1 on, red.
# PB1 grounds Q1's: green. The LEDs sit in the emitters, which keeps the off side's base, fed
# from the on side's low collector, safely below turning on. Q2 has its legs bent the other way
# so both bases face the middle. 1M can't switch either side fully on.
