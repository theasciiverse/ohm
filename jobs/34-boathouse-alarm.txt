# The first chip: a CD4011 NAND gate with its two inputs tied together is a NOT gate. The door
# contact holds the inputs at - while the door is shut; a 1M pull-up lifts them to + when it
# opens, the output falls and sinks the LED's current through 470. Shut, the whole thing draws
# 9 uA through the pull-up: about eight years on a 9 V block. Wrong: no pull-up (the inputs float
# and keep their last level, low: never lit), 10k or 100k pull-ups (they work, but drain the
# battery 100 and 10 times faster), the LED and its resistor across the door contact without a
# chip (it works, wasting 19 mA all season), the LED from the output to - (lit the wrong way).
