title: Series and parallel
unlock: two-doors
para: Parts in series sit one after another in the same loop. The same current flows through all of them, and their resistances add: 100 Ω + 150 Ω makes 250 Ω.
para: Parts in parallel sit side by side between the same two points. Each gets the full voltage, and the current splits between the paths.
para: For switches this is logic. Two buttons in series both have to be pressed for the lamp to light (AND). Two in parallel, either one will do (OR).
source: Kirchhoff's current law: the current flowing into a point equals the current flowing out.
