unlock: start
para: The multimeter reads whatever your cursor is on. On a part: the voltage between its two legs, and the current through it. On copper: the voltage from there to the battery's - terminal.
para: The scope draws a reading over time. Clip its probe onto a part with p (or PROBE, or a middle click), then flip a switch or press a button and watch the trace jump.
para: Measuring beats guessing. When a job fails, point at each part in turn and ask: is the current what I expected?
