title: Current
unlock: start
para: Current is electric charge on the move: how much flows past a point each second, measured in amperes (A). A small LED wants about a hundredth of an ampere, 10 milliamps (10 mA).
para: Current only flows round a closed loop: out of the battery's + terminal, through the parts, and back into its - terminal. Break the loop anywhere and it stops everywhere at once.
para: On the board, current shows as light running along the copper, faster where more flows.
source: SI brochure, BIPM: the ampere is one coulomb of charge per second.
