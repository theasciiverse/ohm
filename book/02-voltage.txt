title: Voltage
unlock: start
para: Voltage is the push that drives current round the loop, measured in volts (V). A battery holds its + terminal a fixed number of volts above its - terminal: about 1.5 V for one AA cell, 4.5 V for three in a row.
para: Voltage is always between two points. The multimeter reads from the point under your cursor to the battery's - terminal, so copper joined to + reads the full battery and copper joined to - reads nothing.
para: Each part in the loop takes a share of the push. Their shares add up to the battery's voltage.
source: SI brochure, BIPM: the volt is one joule of energy per coulomb of charge.
