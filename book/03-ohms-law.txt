title: Ohm's law
unlock: start
para: A resistor resists current. Its resistance, in ohms (Ω), sets how much current a voltage can push through it: current = voltage / resistance, or I = V / R.
para: 4.5 V across 220 Ω gives 4.5 / 220 = 0.020 A, 20 mA. Double the resistance and the current halves; double the voltage and it doubles.
para: The same law turned round tells you the voltage across a resistor from the current through it: V = I × R. Point the multimeter at R1 and check.
source: Georg Simon Ohm, Die galvanische Kette, mathematisch bearbeitet (1827).
